# Privacy for zahranm.cloud

## Scope

This page describes the public hub at zahranm.cloud. Individual project sites may have their own behavior and privacy terms.

## Data collected by this hub

The hub is a static nginx site. It does not provide accounts, a server-side form, a payment flow, or an authenticated data store. Fonts are self-hosted, so no third-party font requests are made. The browser may keep a short-lived local cache of public blog-feed items for up to 15 minutes. That cache remains in the visitor's browser. The home page also builds a "your visit" trace of the demos you touch. It is held only in the tab's memory: never stored or sent, and gone when you leave the page.

## Analytics

Every page loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID `G-P8D6TF0P1C`) to count visits and see which pages are read. That sends the page view to google-analytics.com and stores the usual `_ga` cookies in the browser. Every other asset on the site is self-hosted.

## External services

Besides its own self-hosted assets, Google Analytics, and the public RSS feed from blog.zahranm.cloud, the home page makes no third-party requests. The recruiter copilot at /recruit is a separate service: job descriptions submitted to it and the pitches it generates are logged, and visitor IP addresses are stored only as hashes. To write a pitch, it sends the submitted job description to a third-party LLM API. Don't paste confidential material into it. Project links, GitHub, and LinkedIn are external destinations with their own policies.
