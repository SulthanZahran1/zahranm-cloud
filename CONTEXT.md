# CONTEXT.md

Glossary of domain terms. Implementation-free; see `docs/agents/domain.md` for consumer rules.

- **hub** — the zahranm.cloud apex page: a hand-crafted static index that is the central front door to all public properties.
- **property** — a public web app or site on a zahranm.cloud subdomain, linked from the hub.
- **admin surface** — internal tools (chat, gateway, desktop, dokploy, browser, expense admin) that are deliberately NOT listed on the hub.
- **personal corner** — labs.zahranm.cloud, the user's private/personal lab shelf; untouched by this effort.
- **blog** — the WriteFreely instance at blog.zahranm.cloud, single-user, web-editor driven.
- **recruiter copilot** — the Recruiter Pitch Generator served at zahranm.cloud/recruit by the jd-pitcher service; kept live through the hub cutover.
